YOUR APP LOGO / ICON (optional)

Put your picture here as   logo.jpg   (or logo.png) and rebuild. Nothing else to change.

  - Square works best, 512x512 up to 1024x1024.
  - It becomes the home-screen icon and the logo at the top of the launcher.
  - Keep the important part away from the very edges: round icon masks trim the corners.
  - A picture can't follow the phone's "themed icon" colours, so that variant is switched off.

With no logo here the built-in wheel mark is used. This README is not shipped inside the APK.
