YOUR ART (all optional) - drop files here, rebuild, done. Exact names:

  background.jpg  (or .png / .webp)   STILL picture behind the whole launcher (landscape, 1920x1080 is great).
                                      The background is always a still picture, never a video.
  poster.mp4                          your looping animation. It fills the WHOLE left panel behind the
                                      title and FIGHT button (muted, H.264). A tall 9:16 clip works best:
                                      it is scaled to the panel width and centred, losing only a little
                                      top and bottom. A 16:9 clip would lose the sides instead. Alternatives: poster.gif / poster.webp (animated),
                                      or a still poster.png / poster.jpg.
  layout.json                         your default on-screen control layout. Make it in the app:
                                      Controls > Edit layout > Save file, then put the file here.
  icons/                              your own control icons (see icons/README.txt)

With nothing here: a built-in dark green gradient, and the game's cover art with rising energy sparks.
